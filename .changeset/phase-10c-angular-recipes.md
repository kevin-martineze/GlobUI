Introduces `@glob-ui/angular` (Angular 21 and 22, standalone components and signals): the Dialog and Sheet families, the layout primitives, `AppShellLayout`, `PageLayout` with `PageHeader`, and `StackedLayout`. Each family is a secondary entry point, for example `@glob-ui/angular/dialog`. The class strings shared by the layouts and dialogs moved into `@glob-ui/recipes`. The rendered classes are unchanged in React, Svelte and Vue.
