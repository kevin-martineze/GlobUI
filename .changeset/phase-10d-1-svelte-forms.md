Adds the form and primitive components to `@glob-ui/svelte`: Button, Badge, Label, Input, Textarea, Field, Checkbox, Switch, RadioGroup, Separator, Spinner, Skeleton, Card, Alert, Avatar with AvatarGroup, Progress, Slider, Toggle with ToggleGroup, and Segmented. Each family is a subpath, for example `@glob-ui/svelte/field`. The class strings these components share moved into `@glob-ui/recipes`. The rendered classes are unchanged in React.
